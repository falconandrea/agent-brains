# Compose mode

Use this branch when producing new prose rather than editing an existing draft.
Apply the main skill's voice and factual-preservation rules while writing. The
only deliverable is the requested text, with unresolved factual questions noted
briefly when necessary; no mandatory draft/audit/final sequence.

Before drafting, identify the audience, content type, intended length and source
material. When writing under someone's name, read an available sample or nearby
paragraphs and retain concrete voice signals: vocabulary, sentence-length habits,
directness, uncertainty and punctuation. Without samples use a plain register.

Distinguish specifics supplied by the user, specifics verified in an opened
source, and missing specifics. Link researched claims to their sources. A
remembered version, statistic or quotation is not a verified source. Write
around missing details where possible; use the main skill's TK protocol only
when the missing detail is essential. Preserve requested depth and length.

State the claim directly. Replace generic praise or theatrical framing with
evidence or a practical consequence. Vary sentence and paragraph shape as the
material requires; do not manufacture rhythm or random asymmetry to evade a
detector. A genuine three-item enumeration, domain term or useful contrast is
valid. Calibrate to genre: reference documentation needs navigation, marketing
copy may sell, academic text may hedge.

Read the draft once for unsupported claims, repeated empty framing, voice
mismatch and missing substance. Correct those issues directly. Ordinary short
drafts do not require the optional scanner; counts are not a writing target.

Preventive branch inspired by Alfonso Graziano's
[write-without-slop](https://github.com/alfonsograziano/skills/tree/6dc8f1d7cd7ce59b8b65b693f05050c9e7b70270/skills/write-without-slop).
